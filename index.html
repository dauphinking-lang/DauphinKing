About this tracker

Transactions come from the MJHL 2026-27 transactions page. A GitHub Action re-reads that page every 3 hours and updates this site's data. An open page checks for new data every 10 minutes.

How morale is rated

  • Teams are ranked by total transactions.
  • The busiest third of the league gets Low, the middle third Mid, and the quietest third High. Tied teams share a tier.
  • It's a fun indicator based only on trade volume, not a real measure of the dressing room.

Reading the data

On the MJHL page, each row lists a team and the players that team sends, followed by the other team and what it sends back. "PDF" and "Future Considerations" aren't counted as players. The source page has occasional typos and duplicate rows, so totals can be off by one or two.

About this tracker

Transactions come from the official 2026-27 transactions page. A GitHub Action re-reads that page every 3 hours and updates this site's data. An open page checks for new data every 10 minutes.

Traded-out stats

This tab counts only games played after a player left Dauphin, with his new team. These numbers come from several different sources and aren't refreshed automatically. They're kept in stats.js, and each line is marked Current, May be behind, Partial or Not found. Update a line when you see new numbers, and add one after each new trade.

Reading the data

On the transactions page, each row lists a team and the players that team sends, followed by the other team and what it sends back. "PDF" and "Future Considerations" aren't counted as players. The source page has occasional typos and duplicate rows, so a count can occasionally be off by one.
